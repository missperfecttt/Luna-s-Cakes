Terms of Service

Last Updated: August 2026


1. Order Lead Times

All custom cake orders require a minimum of 48 hours notice. Larger tier cakes and event orders (30+ servings) require at least 5 days notice.


2. Cancellations & Modifications

Orders may be modified or canceled up to 48 hours before the scheduled delivery/pickup time for a full refund. Cancellations made within 48 hours of the scheduled time are non-refundable, as ingredients will have already been prepared.


3. Allergy Disclaimer

While we take severe precautions to avoid cross-contamination, our bakery processes nuts, dairy, wheat, and eggs. We cannot guarantee that any product is 100% free of these allergens. Please inform us of any severe allergies prior to placing your order.


4. Payments

All orders must be paid in full to confirm the booking. We accept Telebirr and Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE) transfers. An order is not considered confirmed until payment verification is complete.


5. Delivery Liability

For delivery orders, Hanit Bakery assumes responsibility for the cake until it is handed over at the delivery address. Once the cake is delivered and accepted, we are not liable for any damage that occurs due to mishandling, transportation, or improper storage (e.g., exposure to heat).
