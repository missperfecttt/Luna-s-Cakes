Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026


1. Information We Collect

When you place an order with Hanit Bakery, we collect the following personal information: Full Name, Phone Number, Email Address (if provided), and Delivery Address (if applicable).


2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to process and fulfill your cake orders. We use your phone number to coordinate delivery or pickup, and your email to send order confirmations and receipts.


3. Data Protection

We take the security of your personal data seriously. Order details are transmitted securely and stored in our internal order management system (Telegram backend) only accessible by Hanit Bakery staff. We do not store sensitive payment information on our servers; all payments are handled securely via third-party banking apps (Telebirr, CBE).


4. Third-Party Sharing

We absolutely do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. We only share necessary delivery addresses with our trusted delivery partners specifically to fulfill your order.
